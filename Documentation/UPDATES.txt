CURRENT TASK: MEMORIAL GUARD COMBAT

Implemented in code (Unity playtest still required):
1. MemorialGuardAI now has Patrol, Chase, BackDash, Attack, LongAttack and AfterAttack states.
2. MG_BackDash is used both as a dodge and as the retreat before MG_LongAttack.
3. MG_LongAttack is a forward lunge with timed Rigidbody2D movement.
4. Ground and wall checks are used before/during dash and lunge movement to avoid intentionally crossing ledges or walls.

7. PlayerHealth.cs adds player HP (default 5), damage reception, temporary invulnerability, damage flash, and disables PlayerMovement/PlayerCombat at 0 HP.
8. Animation events were added to the clips:
   - MG_Attack1.anim: AnimationEvent_MeleeHit at 0.53333336 seconds.
   - MG_LongAttack.anim: AnimationEvent_LongAttackHit at 0.4 seconds.
   These timestamps are initial placements and MUST be checked against the actual weapon-contact frames in Unity's Animation window.
